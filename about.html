About Match The World

Match The World is an interactive memory game that helps players learn to associate flags, countries, and languages while having fun!

The game improves memory, reinforces geography and language knowledge, and is great for all ages — from curious kids to world-traveling adults.

The idea was to combine simple game mechanics with cultural learning. We believe in games that make learning joyful and engaging.

Created using HTML, CSS, JavaScript — and a globe full of imagination.

🎮 How to Play

  • You’ll see a 4×3 grid of face-down cards.
  • Click on a card to reveal a flag, country name, or language.
  • Try to find its matching pair.
  • If the pair matches, both cards stay flipped and a greeting appears in that language.
  • If they don't match, the cards will flip back — try again!
  • When all matches are found, you've won!

Each correct match plays a happy sound and shows a friendly greeting. The goal is to match all pairs while learning something new!
